my apology

Dear Love,

I’m sorry for that moment when you told me you were hurting and overwhelmed.

I was tired, but when I realized you were hurting, I panicked. I got worried about you, and without realizing it, I raised my voice and called you by your name… I’m sorry if the way I reacted made you feel hurt, scared, or like I was angry at you.

I never wanted to be angry at you. I was just so worried that I didn’t know how to handle my fear in that moment. I should have stayed calm and comforted you instead of letting my panic come out that way.

And when you asked me to get angry again afterward, it honestly made me feel even more guilty, because I didn’t want that moment to become something that hurt you. I don’t want my love for you to ever feel like anger or fear.

I’m sorry, Love. I’m still learning how to handle my emotions when I’m scared for someone I love, but I promise I’ll keep trying to do better. You deserve to be comforted gently, especially when you’re already having a hard time.

I love you, baby. I hope you always remember that behind every worried reaction, there is just a heart that cares about you so deeply and never wants to see you hurting. Please be gentle with yourself and stay safe, okay? You don’t have to go through difficult moments alone. Let me be there for you, even if all I can do is stay beside you and remind you that you’re loved.

always learning, always loving you ♡
🌷
